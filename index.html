        Panera menu guide for 2026

        How the Panera menu is priced, where the best value is, and what to order for lunch, dinner or a tight budget.

        1 How the Panera menu is priced

        The Panera menu is easier to understand than it looks, because many sections use flat prices. Every soup costs about $8.49, and most come in a sourdough bread bowl for about $9.08. Value Duets are a flat $7.99 (one is $7.69). Bagels are $1.99 each and most pastries $4.39. The big price differences are in sandwiches ($7.99–$13.39) and salads ($8.99–$13.59), where chicken, steak and premium toppings add to the cost.

        2 Budget picks under $5

        There are 28 items on our Panera menu list under $5. They are mostly bakery items, sides and drinks: bagels ($1.99), Crème Brûlée Bites ($1.99), cookies ($3.19), muffins and scones ($3.89), Classic Avo toast ($3.89), fountain drinks ($3.39) and hot coffee ($2.99). A bagel with a coffee is one of the cheapest breakfasts on the whole Panera menu at about $4.98 before tax.

        3 Mid-range picks, $5 to $10

        This is where most Panera meals sit. It covers every soup and bread bowl, all the Value Duets, breakfast sandwiches and bagel stacks ($5.39–$6.99), kids' meals ($5.79–$7.19), the Grilled Cheese and Mediterranean Veggie sandwiches ($7.99), and salads without chicken, like the Caesar and Greek ($8.99). For the best value in this tier, go for a Value Duet or a soup in a bread bowl.

        4 Premium picks over $10

        Premium items are mostly toasted sandwiches and chicken salads. Think of the Ciabatta Cheesesteak ($13.39), Green Goddess Chicken Cobb ($13.59), Toasted Italiano ($12.99), Grilled Chicken & Avo BLT ($12.99), the Bacon Turkey Bravo ($11.99) and Mac & Cheese ($10.59–$11.79). They are bigger, single-item meals. Pair one with water or a coffee to keep the total down.

        5 The Panera lunch menu

        At lunch, the Panera menu centres on sandwiches, soups, salads and Value Duets. A lunch with a full sandwich and a fountain drink is about $11–$17 before tax. A Value Duet with iced tea is about $11.38. Office regulars often rotate between a Duet, a bread bowl and a salad to keep costs steady through the week, which is exactly what our lunch planner does. Lunch hours, combos and the full midday line-up are on the Panera Bread Lunch Menu page.

        6 Gluten-conscious choices

        Panera is a bakery, so most of its menu contains wheat. Some items are made without gluten-containing ingredients, such as some salads ordered without croutons or bread, and some soups. Panera does not describe its cafes as gluten-free environments, and cross-contact can happen in a kitchen where bread is baked all day. If you need to avoid gluten, check the allergen details in the Panera app and ask the team before ordering. Options and the latest information are collected on the Panera Gluten Free Menu page.

        7 Panera for dinner

        Many cafes stay open into the evening, and the same Panera menu works well for dinner. A bread bowl or Mac & Cheese is filling on its own, while a Family Feast (about $36, or $41 with cookies) feeds a household without ordering item by item. Soups by the quart (about $21.99) are a good way to stock up for later in the week.

        8 Panera on a student budget

        With a budget of about $10, the smartest Panera menu choices are a Value Duet ($7.99), a soup on its own ($8.49) or a bagel and a coffee ($4.98). Adding the Unlimited Sip Club ($14.99 a month or $99 a year) makes sense if you study in the cafe and refill drinks often. MyPanera rewards also bring member offers and a birthday treat.

        9 Breakfast on the Panera menu

        The morning Panera menu has bagel stacks and soufflés (about $6.99), ciabatta and croissant egg sandwiches ($6.89), a plain Egg & Cheese ($5.39), oatmeal ($6.19) and parfaits ($5.89). Pastries and bagels are sold all day.

        10 Bakery and bagels on the Panera menu

        The bakery case is the cheapest corner of the Panera menu. Single bagels are about $1.99, a Half Dozen about $9.29 and a Baker's Dozen about $13.99. Many cafes offer a Tuesday Baker's Dozen for about $9.99. Cookies start at $3.19, pastries such as the Cinnamon Roll and Pecan Braid are about $4.39, and whole loaves like the Country Rustic Sourdough are about $8.79.

        11 The Panera kids menu

        Kids' meals on the Panera menu cost about $5.79 for Grilled Cheese, $6.79 for Broccoli Cheddar Soup, $6.99 for Mac & Cheese and $7.19 for a Turkey or Ham & Cheese sandwich. Add an apple ($1.39) or milk ($2.49) for a full kids' lunch under $10.

        12 Why prices vary

        Panera bakery-cafes are run by the company and by franchise partners, so prices follow local costs. Airports, city centres and delivery apps usually cost the most. The Panera menu prices on this page are typical U.S. prices, last checked in November 2026.

        Panera menu FAQ

        Quick answers about Panera menu prices and options.

        What is the cheapest thing on the Panera menu?

        The cheapest items are a French Baguette side (about $0.99), chips or an apple ($1.39) and a bagel or Crème Brûlée Bite ($1.99). The cheapest meal is a Value Duet at about $7.69 to $7.99.

        How much is lunch at Panera?

        A Panera lunch with a full sandwich and a fountain drink is about $11 to $17 before tax. A Value Duet with iced tea is about $11.38, and a soup with a drink about $11.88.

        What is the most expensive item on the Panera menu?

        On the regular menu, the Green Goddess Chicken Cobb salad (about $13.59) and the Ciabatta Cheesesteak (about $13.39) are the most expensive single items.

        Are Panera soups all the same price?

        Yes, at most bakery-cafes. Each soup costs about $8.49, or about $9.08 in a sourdough bread bowl. Mac & Cheese is priced separately at about $10.59.

        Is the Panera menu the same at every location?

        The core Panera menu is the same across the U.S., but prices, seasonal items and some limited offers can differ from one bakery-cafe to another.

        Does Panera have gluten-free options?

        Panera has some items made without gluten-containing ingredients, but it does not describe its cafes as gluten-free. Check the allergen details in the Panera app and ask the team if you need to avoid gluten.

        How can I plan cheap Panera lunches for the week?

        Use the weekly lunch planner on this page. Set your budget and lunch style, and it builds a Monday-to-Friday plan with a different main each day and the total cost.
